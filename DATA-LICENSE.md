# Data sources and licensing

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers project software. The source material below has its own provenance and terms.

| Source | Scope and terms |
| --- | --- |
| [Original Google Maps export](data/mutta-puffs-everywhere.csv) | Preserved crowdsourced export. Source terms and contributor rights apply. The project does not assign an additional license to the archived material. |
| Natural Earth 5.1.2 | Public-domain administrative boundaries. The map outline is simplified from this dataset. [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) |
| geoBoundaries India ADM2 | District geometry from Pathways Data Pvt. Ltd. and the Local Government Directory, represented as of 2021. The [repackaged district snapshot](data/reference/geoboundaries-ind-adm2-9469f09.json.gz) and source district data are available under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). [Boundary metadata](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IND/ADM2/) |
| Independently approved contributions | Original contribution text and first-hand observations can be shared under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) when the contributor confirms it. The linked issue or PR supplies attribution. Licensed photos retain their recorded credit and license. |

The versioned exports retain each place’s origin and coordinate accuracy. Review and observation records link to their own source. Review text, photos, prices, and ratings from commercial map listings are outside the import.

The [homepage image](public/images/egg-puff.webp) is generated editorial artwork. It does not depict a listed bakery.
