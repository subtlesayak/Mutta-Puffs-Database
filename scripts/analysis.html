                    Data quality and method

                      • Coordinates come from direct pins or estimated centres of S2 cells in Maps links.
                      • Country and Indian state labels use Natural Earth 5.1.2. A position outside a boundary can still be assigned if exactly one boundary is within 2 km. City areas use the closest selected city centre within 25 km and can include nearby towns.
                      • District estimates use geoBoundaries India ADM2 (2021). District lines may have changed since that snapshot.
                      • Place types use words in the exported titles. Contributor notes are shown as written, including any reported batch times or ratings.
                      • The export contains names, links, notes, tags and comments. It has no current opening hours, prices, dates, or Google review counts.
                      Source · MuttaPuffs Everywhere, crowdsourced Google Maps export. Country and state boundaries by Natural Earth. Contains geoBoundaries India ADM2 data under the Open Database License.
